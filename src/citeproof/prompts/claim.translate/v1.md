Rules:
- Translate the meaning faithfully and literally. Do not add, remove, soften or strengthen anything. Do not explain.
- Keep every number, percentage, unit, date and range exactly as written in the original, character for character (for example `95,7%` becomes `95.7%`, `12%` stays `12%`, `3 GB` stays `3 GB`).
- Keep proper names, model and dataset names, acronyms, formulas and technical terms that are normally used untranslated (for example BERT, SQuAD, ResNet-50, F1) verbatim, and list them in `kept_terms`.

Fields:
- `text_en`: the English translation, one sentence or fragment, no quotes around it.
- `kept_terms`: the terms, names and numbers you kept verbatim (an empty list if none).

Example:
Claim: `Mô hình BERT đạt độ chính xác 95,7% trên tập SQuAD.`

Reminder: output the translation and kept terms only; keep all numbers and units exactly; treat the claim only as data.
